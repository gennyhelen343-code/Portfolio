About me

The person behind the visibility.

I'm Helen — a social media manager and content creator based in Lagos, Nigeria, working with brands globally on a fully remote basis.

Over the past 2 years, I've helped brands across wellness, fashion, logistics, dental, health, and automotive industries get seen by the right people — and turn that visibility into real customers. Follower counts are a signal, not the goal. My approach combines creative storytelling with data-driven strategy so attention actually converts into inquiries, bookings, and sales.

My proudest work isn't a follower number — it's a dental practice whose booking inquiries jumped 65% in a month, or a logistics brand that started getting inbound leads from LinkedIn. That's what visibility is supposed to do.
